Am I the only one bothered by fake enthusiasm?

I come across tons of content, here on LinkedIn and on other platforms, where the creator of the day gets excited about every little thing.

An indie game comes out with original but very questionable gameplay?
"It's insanely awesome!"

Some random guy implements the thousandth personal finance webapp with Claude?
"Finally a solution that will change everything!"

The hundredth AI model of the year comes out?
"We've reached AGI!" (for at least the tenth time this year xD)

The most infuriating part? That this content often really works. 

And so I admit the thought of trying it crossed my mind.
The problem is that I feel like a hypocrite even while writing it, and I end up not publishing it.
