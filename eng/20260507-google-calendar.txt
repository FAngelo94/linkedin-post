Recently I added the ability to book appointments with me on my personal website.
A feature that in reality is barely used — people usually just reach out to me directly on LinkedIn.

When I started looking for a tool, I had one main requirement: it needed to sync with my personal Google Calendar to avoid overlapping events.

And that’s exactly where I discovered a Google Calendar feature I had never noticed before: “Booking pages”, which allows you to define availability across days and time slots and share it via a simple link.

I’ve been using Google Calendar for years, but since I had never needed it, that feature had simply stayed hidden from me until now.

Has it ever happened to you to discover a feature in a software you’ve been using for a long time only when you actually needed it?
