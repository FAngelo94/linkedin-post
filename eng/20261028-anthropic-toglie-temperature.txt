With the new API version of October 21, 2026, Anthropic removed temperature, top_p and top_k.

First of all, what were they for?
In short, they were used to control how "creative" or "rigid" the model's behavior was.

And in their place?
Nothing.
There's effort, but it controls how much the model reasons, not how creative it is.

The reason?
Officially I haven't found it written anywhere.
In my opinion, and it's the thing I find most interesting about the news, frontier models have become so complex that not even their creators can predict them.
As a result, introducing parameters to control their behavior is becoming harder and harder.


Personally I never used those parameters in my most important projects so it's a change that didn't affect me.
If it caused problems for any of you, I'd like to know how you handled the situation.
