I organized my second trip to Japan starting from the diary of the first.

From a certain point in my life onward I picked up this habit.
Both for trips and for the projects I work on.

What is it for?
On trips, to remember details I would forget over time.
On projects, to find tools, resources or solutions used in the past.

The only problem?
The more the diary grows, the harder it gets to find anything in it.

With AI this problem has practically disappeared.

- Japan: I had to go back to Kyoto and Tokyo and wanted to see new places. I attached the diary of the first trip and asked it to exclude the stops already visited
- Work: I needed a tool I had already used but whose name I didn't remember. I explained the problem to Claude, attached the diary, and it found it for me

It's like having an external memory I can finally ask questions to.
