Best strategy to save tokens? Simplicity.
LinkedIn is full of posts selling prompt tricks and token hacks. Some work, but often simplifying the problem saves tokens naturally.
Practical tips:
- Use smaller models for simple tasks. Don’t take the car for a 200-meter trip.
- Don’t load skills or MCPs you don’t need for the task.
- Start a new session for a new, unrelated task instead of carrying a heavy context.
- After a long planning session, ask the model for a short summary of todo and key points to reload into a new session.
