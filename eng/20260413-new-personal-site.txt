Personal Site Updated

After finishing a collaboration with a client I decided to spend some time improving my personal site, to update it but also to make it more useful and interesting to potential new clients or collaborators.

The changes come from three main sources:
- feedback I received
- some colleagues' sites on LinkedIn
- an analysis done by Claude, which gave me several concrete ideas to work on.

Main updates:
- Reorganized the site into 3 pages instead of 1
- I revised the homepage, adding more useful information for potential clients, such as delivery times and past client reviews
- Reluctantly I removed, at least for now, the animated background — it was a divisive feature, appreciated by some but less by others
- Dedicated a page to past works and projects, creating a section for the main projects to which I also added less-technical descriptions
- I added a small blog to collect what I publish on LinkedIn and to make the site more updatable and indexable
- Added a chatbot where you can ask information about me (for the lazier HRs xD)
