How strong is Opus 5.5, really?

It came out last week and after less than 24 hours I was already seeing posts praising it.

I, in all honesty, wanted to try it for a few days, and on different tasks, before expressing a judgment.

So?
I really did notice a huge leap in quality compared to the previous version, in two points in particular:
- in coding, it seems to use far fewer tokens
- in the posts it helps me write, the quality is on a whole other level

For example, while reviewing a post, without my asking, it came up on its own with a metaphor that fit perfectly with what I had written.

For those using it, in which use case have you found it best?
