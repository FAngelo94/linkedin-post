Predictive AI is the real superpower of AI

When AI is discussed, even here in the posts I see on LinkedIn, it's almost always in terms of generative AI

It's used to automate tasks, develop faster, write emails

However I read that, data in hand, the real difference for companies in terms of the bottom line is being made by Predictive AI

In short, the real advantage of using AI isn't producing more stuff faster, but deciding better

This is because it makes it possible, by analyzing all the available data, to make predictions about your customers, suppliers or products

Something that until recently required entire teams of analysts and weeks or months of work can now be done in a few hours, a few days at most
