To organize my daily tasks I use Notepad.

It almost makes me laugh to write it, given there are dozens of well-made web apps that do the same thing.
But on this I stay minimalist.

Blank sheet.
One line per task.
An X on the completed ones.

For medium to long term planning I use more structured tools.
For example I get along well with GitHub Projects.

But day to day, for me, Notepad remains unbeatable.
