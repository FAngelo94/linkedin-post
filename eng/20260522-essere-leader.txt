What's one of the most important tasks of a leader?
I think it's creating cohesion among the people they lead.

Can you imagine a team leader who sets developers against each other?
And I'm not talking about competition, which can even be healthy if managed properly.
I mean sowing discord or, as we say today, fake news, to make them fight and create factions.

The crazy thing is that this is now the most common kind of attitude from our political class.
And I don't mean only in Italy, but pretty much everywhere.
The ones who should be the leaders par excellence, being at the helm of a country, instead of uniting, divide to gain more consensus.

And every event is a good excuse to immediately sow hatred and division, even by inventing facts or distorting reality.
To create narratives of an "us" and a "them", which can change depending on need.

And then, is it the fault of politicians or of voters who actually reward this kind of behavior?
