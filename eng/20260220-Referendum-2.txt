A thought on the Justice Referendum – part 2

If a site's users use it differently than developers expected, or they can do things they "shouldn't be able to do" because of a bug, who is responsible?

The users who "didn't understand"?
Or those who designed and developed the system?

In software the answer is quite clear:
if the behavior is technically possible, the responsibility lies with whoever wrote the rules of the game.

This reasoning came back to me while reading, these days, the government's attacks on the judiciary, accused of "obstructing" political action.

But the judiciary applies the laws.
And who writes the laws?

If a system produces unwanted effects, maybe the problem isn't who runs it, but who designed it.

In tech we call it a design flaw.
In politics what do we call it?
