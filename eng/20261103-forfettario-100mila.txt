The flat-rate regime rises to 100,000 euros.

On October 28th the Council of Ministers approved the 2027 budget bill: the revenue threshold to stay in the regime goes from 85,000 to 100,000 euros, substitute tax always at 15% (5% for new businesses).

The VAT exemption however stays at 85,000, because that cap is set by a European directive.
Whoever is between 85 and 100 thousand remains flat-rate, but with VAT on invoices and the related obligations, with procedures still to be defined.

Who is it for?
In July I shared the data on the distribution of flat-rate taxpayers by revenue:
- 46% under 20 thousand euros
- 36% between 20 and 50 thousand
- 13% between 50 and 80 thousand
- 4% over 80 thousand

It intervenes on a slice that sits entirely inside that 4%.
(Unfortunately I'm not in that slice, so the news doesn't change anything for me xD)

I wonder whether this move will make happier those between 85 and 100 thousand euros in revenue.
Maybe helping them with a less "dramatic" shift toward the ordinary regime.

Or whether it will bother ordinary VAT holders more.
Among whom I had read posts about the "unfair competition" of flat-rate taxpayers.

What do you think?
