Is it possible to update the voting method in Italy?

Last weekend, like many, I went to vote in the referendum and, like every vote, it seems absurd in 2026 to still be there with paper and pencil to vote.
Is it possible that we can't set up a more digitized system?

We already have a way to authenticate ourselves at the state level, SPID or, even better, the CIE.

A monitor in each polling station would be enough, or maybe even poll workers' smartphones.
You authenticate with SPID or CIE, scan a QR code that regenerates every few seconds and you vote.
The vote ends up in an encrypted blockchain that is decrypted only when the polls close.

I would still always have people go to a physical location to vote (not necessarily their registered address at this point) to be sure they don't suffer threats during voting

I don't understand why this topic isn't discussed more often and why we don't start planning its future use.
