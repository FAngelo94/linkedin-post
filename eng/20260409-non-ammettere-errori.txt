Who causes the most damage in a company or project?

Those who can't admit their mistakes.

Not the people who make mistakes — we all do.
But those who make mistakes and keep going, without correcting themselves.

This creates toxic environments, where no one speaks up, no one tells the truth, and problems pile up until they explode.

On my side, when I make a mistake in the projects I work on I say it immediately, to the team or to the client, usually proposing a solution.
I prefer transparency, even at the risk of losing a client.

Now, what happens when such a person surrounds themself with "yes men"?
You don't need to imagine it — just look at the current global political scenario.

Decisions made without critical analysis, no one raising their hand to say "maybe we're wrong", with disastrous consequences for billions of people.
