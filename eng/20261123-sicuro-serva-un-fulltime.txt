Sure you need a full-time developer?

In my experience, many software projects don't require someone full time.

The reason?
Work comes in peaks.

Even when I was an employee I had periods of intense work alternating with periods when I had practically almost nothing to do.
Whether because of the testing phase or waiting for client feedback, there are moments when work slows down considerably.
The salary, though, rightly, arrived all the same.

With AI this has become even more evident.
The peaks have shortened, the quiet periods much less so.

And yet I still see a lot of listings looking for full-time developers.

I'm not saying full-time is never needed, of course.
If software is the heart of your company and you can use a resource across multiple projects, having someone in-house makes sense.

But if you need someone to evolve a single product and be there at peak moments, there are other ways.

What's your take?
And those who hired an in-house developer for a single project, a few months later would you do it again?
