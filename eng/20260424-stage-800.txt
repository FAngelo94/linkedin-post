"Young people today refuse an €800/month internship because they don’t want to work" — context matters.
I read a post reacting to a rejected €800 offer, and the author surprised me by assuming laziness.
800 euros can mean very different things: city of the role, relocation, study background, duration, and growth prospects.
Early in my career I rejected a similar offer in Milan because I had projects and couldn’t make ends meet — context changes the decision.
If you want to criticize a refusal, at least provide context.
