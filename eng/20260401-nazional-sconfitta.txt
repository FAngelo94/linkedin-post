Is the national football team a mirror of the country?

Excellent alone, mediocre together

I take advantage of the bad news about the national team being eliminated from the World Cup (and it's three times in a row but I'm confident the record can be extended) to share a thought, or a question, that occasionally crosses my mind but that actually doesn't find a real answer.

We Italians, taken individually, are exceptional — there's at least one Italian in important positions in almost every research lab in the world, not to mention the excellent companies we have

And yet as a group, as a nation, it seems we can't be at the same level — why?

I more or less have my own idea why but I'd like to know yours, what do you think?
