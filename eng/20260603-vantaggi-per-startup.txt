Dynamic projects? Per-project pricing is a trap.

In innovative contexts requirements change constantly. Estimating everything upfront is often impossible, so freelancers usually choose one of these two paths:

- Raise the initial figure a lot to cover uncertainty.
- Offer a low price but tie the work to the current plan, charging extra for every change.

Both options make costs unpredictable or unsustainable for the startup.

My proposal: a fixed monthly fee (subscription).

How it works:
a monthly fee that covers the activities needed to move the project forward: development, bug fixes, small tweaks, reprioritizations.

Why it's convenient:
- More predictable expenses, often lower.
- Faster iterations: you don't lose time on contracts for every change.
- More flexibility to adapt to changing priorities.

In my experience this model has often reduced costs for clients and sped up development.
