Do LLMs really understand what we say?

Or have they simply become monstrously good at imitating human language?

I read an article that argues an interesting thesis: current LLMs aren't truly intelligent and, above all, will never lead to AGI as they're conceived today.

They don't model understanding.
They model probability.

Basically, a model answers that 2+2=4 not because it "understood" addition, but because that answer is statistically the most plausible.

What do I think?

Today's most advanced models require enormous amounts of data, computing power and energy to replicate, at least in part, abilities that the human brain expresses using just 20 watts.

So is a paradigm shift needed to reach true AGI?
For now I think so.

What's your take?
