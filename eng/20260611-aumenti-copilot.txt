Is it still worth using Copilot after Microsoft's senseless price hikes?
Spoiler: I'm not so sure anymore.

I've used Copilot since the beta and, before these increases, I recommended it for very concrete reasons:

- it was cheap, about 100 euros a year
- inline suggestions that are really handy for developers
- native integration with GitHub, my main development platform
- it let you try different models, even though in recent months the choice had already shrunk
- it also offered free models useful for the simplest tasks

Since June 1st, though, the token count on the main models hasn't simply doubled: in some cases it has gone up 6x, with Sonnet reaching even 9x.

Before, I could cover the month by managing the various models well and using the free ones. Today, with the same usage, I struggle to make it to the end of the first week.

Since my annual subscription will still last for quite a few months, the most sensible solution I've found for now was to add Claude Pro and use the two tools together.

Claude Pro isn't exactly generous with tokens either, so on its own it wouldn't be enough for me. But combined with Copilot, it's still manageable.

On top of that, the former free Copilot models are still very convenient, since they still have a 0.33x count. If you give them a well-split plan of tasks, maybe prepared with Sonnet or Opus, they still do a great job and save quite a few tokens.

In my opinion Microsoft made a blunder raising its costs so much and with so little notice, the risk of pushing people toward Anthropic or other competitors is high.

What do you think?

Or how did you organize yourselves if you used Copilot?
