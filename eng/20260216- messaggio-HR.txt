An invitation to some HRs and headhunters 👇

If you send automated messages to multiple candidates, don’t try to pass them off as personalized with phrases like:
"I’ve reviewed your profile and find you aligned ..."

Because then it happens that:
– I receive offers that have nothing to do with my profile
– I’m asked to send my CV when my profile already contains a link to my personal website with everything needed just below my profile photo (as you can see in the image)

For me it’s fine to automate, managing multiple searches at once, I realize writing one-by-one messages would be unsustainable.

But don’t try to hide it clumsily.

Am I the only one bothered by this?
