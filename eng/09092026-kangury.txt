Meet Kangury, a bridge between physical and digital photos

It's the first startup in which I collaborated as a cofounder together with @emanuele schiavon (who took care more of the business and testing side).

We've been working on it, with ups and downs, for 3 years now and I've learned a huge amount along the way:

- Managing a real project as fullstack as fullstack gets: frontend, backend, db, integration of various external services and deployment of prod and test environments
- Collaborating with a cofounder on all strategic and operational decisions
- Managing suppliers both contractually and operationally, integrating with their systems
- Testing the market and making quick changes to the project
- Dealing with external consultants and potential clients
