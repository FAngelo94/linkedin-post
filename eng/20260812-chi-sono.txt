I've been posting here for a while but I've never introduced myself. Partly for privacy and partly because I prefer talking about work rather than about myself. 

Reading others, though, I became convinced that a bit of context is needed.

So, in chronological order:

I was born in Caltanissetta and, after elementary school, I moved to Sondrio. From one end of Italy to the other, I confess it was a bit traumatic.

I chose the ITIS because I liked math, but the practical kind, so no liceo.

There I wrote my first code using C# and then Java. Questionable choices as first languages? Maybe, but I wasn't the teacher.

From there the move to the Politecnico di Milano was natural. I loved Milan as a student as much as I hated it as a worker, in fact I didn't stay long.

During my student years I worked on several projects, here are the ones I remember most fondly:

- the submarine mentioned above: it was my high school thesis, built with salvaged materials and zero budget;
- scala 40: the first game I developed for Android;
- two robots in multidisciplinary projects, for which I handled the software and electronics side;
- a recommendation algorithm, in collaboration with Spotify;
- a route optimization algorithm.

I'd like to dig some of them up and tell you about them properly, time permitting and, above all, if you're interested.
