In Japan cash is still needed, but not where I thought

Curiosities from Japan - part 2

During my first trip I was surprised to discover that in many places they didn't accept cards.

Even at the machines for buying subway tickets you can only pay in cash (if you don't have the Suica, their rechargeable card).

I thought of Japan as a futuristic country.
In many respects it is, of course.
On cash usage, instead, it's quite similar to Italy.

On this second trip I also visited Hokkaido and I expected cards to be accepted even less there than in Tokyo, Osaka and Kyoto.

Instead, to my surprise, they accepted them almost everywhere, even in the most remote villages.

Did you imagine that?
