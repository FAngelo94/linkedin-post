Where can you find them?
On my personal website, of course

What do you mean? Why did I put them in my portfolio?
In the past I've come across developers' websites built like a video game where you could discover their skills and projects by wandering around a small world in the style of Super Mario.

As a gamer (with less and less time to play 😅) I've always found it an original and fun way to introduce yourself, but I never had the time to actually implement it.

With the release of Claude's Fable model I decided to use it to build it on my portfolio and, in the meantime, test its reasoning and code generation abilities.

So will HR have to play to discover my skills?
Only if they want to, of course. I included it as an optional thing and, for now, it isn't complete, but I still wanted to start sharing it to get some feedback before sinking more time into it.

There are some things about Fable that really surprised me, but those deserve a dedicated and more technical post.

For now, have fun!
