Texas has opened an investigation into LinkedIn.

The reason: LinkedIn sells Premium Career at $39.99/month and Premium Business at $69.99/month to job seekers, without saying that a substantial part of the listings are "ghost jobs".

That is, positions that don't really exist. 
And that the company has no intention of filling.

According to the AG's office, we're talking about 20-27% of the listings on the platform. 
One in four.

It's not the first time ghost jobs have been discussed. But it's the first time someone with legal power has tried to ask LinkedIn to account for how much it knew and what it said (or didn't say) to its subscribers.

Now I don't know if the situation is the same here in Italy too.
But I periodically take a look at job listings on LinkedIn and, talking with other people who use it for work, compared to a few years ago some things have changed:
1) There are fewer listings in general
2) Several listings seem to repeat identically in cycles for the same companies (which really makes me suspect they're ghost jobs)
3) Fewer replies to applications sent
