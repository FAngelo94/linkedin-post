Do you struggle to translate your app into tasks to give to the AI?
Try Kiro AWS.

It's a development environment created by Amazon, seemingly very similar to Visual Studio Code, with which it shares the open source base.
Unlike Visual Studio Code, though, Kiro is designed for a more agentic use.

Before writing code Kiro analyzes your requirements written in natural language and produces specification and design documents that you can review and edit before moving on to writing the code.
This way, Kiro helps you turn your ideas into concrete tasks.

Out of curiosity I tried it and here's what I think.

I find it a valid tool for people without technical skills, neither as a developer nor in AI.
You can even use it from the browser without installing anything, if you want.

As a developer, though, I found it too verbose. What it does is easy to replicate with Claude/Copilot by giving them the right instructions or integrating the right skill.
