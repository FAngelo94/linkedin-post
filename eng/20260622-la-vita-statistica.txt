"Work hard and you'll succeed."

It's one of the most toxic messages going around on LinkedIn.

Because it's false and, when things go wrong, it puts all the blame on whoever tried.

The reality is more uncomfortable:
effort + dedication + whatever you want does not equal success

If it were that simple, the world would be full of successful startups, millionaire companies and established professionals.

This is not an invitation to give up.
It's an invitation not to tell yourselves fairy tales.

The more effort you put into something, the more your chances of a good result go up. 
But a probability is not a guarantee.

Remembering it matters because it helps you handle failures better, learn from mistakes and not turn every negative result into a personal fault.

That said, if you're working on an idea or a startup and want to increase the chances of turning it into something concrete, write to me.
