A client who stays or a client who comes back?
Which one gives you more satisfaction?

Because for a few days now I've been working again with a client I had worked with a few months ago.
A startup, by the way.

Of course, the client who stays over time and renews their trust in me every month is gold.
But the one who disappears for months and then comes back carries enormous weight too.

So I couldn't choose between the two.
