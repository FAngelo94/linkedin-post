I admit the No representatives convinced me more, so for now that's what I'll vote

Then beyond what is said in the videos my two main reasons for No are:
1) As already said in a previous post, I find it nonsensical to delegate such a technical choice to citizens; let the politicians take the responsibilities for which they are handsomely paid
2) Did we seriously need this referendum? With all the problems Italy has, whether Yes or No wins the truth is that it won't change anything for most Italians, so my vote is also a No; this is not the priority the government should have, there are many other important things
