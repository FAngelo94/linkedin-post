Browsers for humans are shutting down and browsers for agents are being born

I got to thinking while reading these two pieces of news at the same time:

- On August 9th OpenAI shut down ChatGPT Atlas, a browser launched less than ten months ago

- Meanwhile, on August 6th, Cloudflare announced Kitesurf, a browser with no UI (sorry, frontend developers), built to optimize agentic browsing

Cloudflare's choice, which of the two is the one I find more interesting, comes from the observation that automation has now surpassed humans in web browsing: 57.5% of HTTP requests to web content come from automated agents, against 42.5% from human users.

Who knows how web development will evolve in the coming years, what do you think? 
Are you already building your browsers with an eye on agents?
