Curiosities from Japan - 1

I already knew this from my first trip, but it always leaves me speechless.

Almost nobody speaks English.
And the few who do know very little.

And I'm not talking about some remote village.
I'm talking about hotel receptionists in huge, touristy cities like Kyoto or Tokyo.

So how do you understand each other?

Some pull out small portable translators that get it right one time out of three.
I use Google Translate and I wonder why they don't use it too 😅

If you've been there, did you have the same impression?
And if you live or work there, can you explain why?
