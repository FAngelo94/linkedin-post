Customizable theme or brand identity first?

I have a friend who is obsessed with custom themes on websites. 

Every time he builds a new site, the first thing he does is implement the feature to choose among no fewer than ten different themes.

And he also complains that most sites don't offer this option.

On one hand I myself appreciate being able to choose among different themes, almost always landing on the dark one like a good developer.

On the other hand though I think it's important for a brand to have its own recognizable colors and that, from this point of view, a customizable theme could be counterproductive.

What's your take?
