On LinkedIn you can follow a person without having to add them to your contacts.

For a few months now I've decided to take better care of my LinkedIn network.

How?
I write to every new contact I add or accept, just to introduce ourselves.

The problem?
Some don't reply, not even after days.
And so I remove them.

Not because I take offense, of course.
But because I prefer a smaller network of people who feel like exchanging a few words.

If you like my content, and it makes me really happy, but you don't want me to "bother" you privately, there's the Follow button.
