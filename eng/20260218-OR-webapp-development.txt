🚀 Orbiting Rocks is online

Cybersecurity is not (only) protection. It’s legibility.

Early-stage startups live in constant acceleration.
They build while deciding. They decide while fundraising. They fundraise while being analyzed.

The problem?
Trust breaks before systems break.

Orbiting Rocks flips the traditional way of looking at security in startups.

It doesn’t talk about checklists.
It doesn’t sell theatrical compliance.
It doesn’t propose SOC 2 to a pre-seed.

It talks about Trust Legibility.

👉 Not “how secure are you?”
👉 But “are you legible under scrutiny?”

For investors, enterprise partners and regulators one thing matters:
understanding how you think, how you decide, how you manage risk.

Orbiting Rocks works precisely on this:
- makes explicit decisions that usually remain implicit
- structures responsibilities before they become chaos
- transforms technical trade-offs into a defensible narrative
- prepares the startup for due diligence before it arrives

They also have a free tool, the Trust Legibility Tool, which in 10 minutes tells you:
- where your security posture is interpretable
- where it risks creating friction
- what to say (and what to avoid) during due diligence

In early-stage, the difference is not made by who has more policies.
It’s made by those who know how to explain why they exist, or why they don’t yet.
