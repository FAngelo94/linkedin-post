AI hasn't truly revolutionized companies yet.

When electricity arrived, a revolution similar to AI today, companies didn't grasp its real potential right away: it was a gradual process that lasted 30 years.

At first electricity was used as an add-on component, but little by little the entire structure of companies changed, redesigning the new production processes around this new technology.

In the same way, AI is starting to be integrated into companies, but its full potential hasn't been understood yet. Many businesses use it to automate repetitive tasks or analyze data, but they haven't yet completely rethought their business models or operating processes around it.
