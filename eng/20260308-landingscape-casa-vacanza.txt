How an agency "sold" me the website for my holiday home

A few months ago I put a holiday home up for rent on Booking.com and Airbnb.

At first I didn't intend to make a dedicated website.
My idea was simple: most people search directly on the portals or on Google Maps, where I added the property with my contacts.

However, last week I started receiving several calls from agencies wanting to sell me a website for my holiday home.
The first few times I politely declined.

After the umpteenth insistent call I decided to explain my point of view: for a small holiday home, in my opinion, having a website isn't that essential.

The salesperson didn't agree.

He explained that, from their experience (it's their job after all), many people search on Google even to look for holiday homes and having a website is very important.

Well, he convinced me, so I told him I would develop the site myself, since it's my job, and that if they needed a hand I'd be available to talk about it.

The guy took it well, he laughed and told me they didn't need it at the moment, wishing me a good evening.

Do you really need a website for a case like this? I'm not completely convinced yet, but at least nobody calls me anymore to sell me one. 😄
