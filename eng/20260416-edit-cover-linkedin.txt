Want to update your LinkedIn cover quickly? 🚀

I admit I didn't feel like using more professional tools like GIMP and hunting for the right dimensions. So, while looking online for a quick solution, I came across Edit.org

After a free signup, just search among the templates for "LinkedIn Cover" to find a wide selection of ready-made templates with the correct dimensions

It's an ideal solution for:
✔️ finding inspiration if you don't know where to start
✔️ easily customizing texts, colors and images
✔️ downloading the final result as PNG or JPG

Here's the link: https://edit.org/
