Last week I completed my first project done for a client completely “externally” (without intermediaries) using the "Subscription Developer" model

How did it work?
- Initial call: they explained goals and requirements.
- Estimate: 2 weeks of development → 2-week subscription.
- The next day: I start working.
- End of week 1: demo of what’s been done. Client satisfied → pays for both weeks.
- End of week 2: site delivered.

Advantages
- You start immediately. Zero time lost to endless contracts and technical interviews. If after the first week they aren’t satisfied, they don’t pay.
- Total flexibility. During the 2 weeks they can ask for changes and tweaks without debates about “how much this change costs”. I only point out if we’re going beyond the 2-week subscription.
- No micromanagement. No timesheets, no estimate for every single change. Focus only on the goal.

Final result?
Client satisfied.
