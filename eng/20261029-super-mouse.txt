Meet the Super Mouse you didn't know you needed

Sometimes, looking at certain products, I wonder "Who came up with this? And, above all, why?"

One of those times was when I saw the article about this mouse.

What can you do with it? Here are some of the features:
- Virtual swipe keyboard
- Up to 16 shortcuts and macros
- Google Calendar
- Voice dictation
- Gyroscope controls

All features I already have at hand on my keyboard.
And for macros I prefer the physical buttons of my mouse.

Ergonomics obviously nonexistent, having to make room for the touchscreen.

The price is the other component that makes me question the real usefulness of this mouse.
It starts at $199, with estimated delivery in February 2027.
