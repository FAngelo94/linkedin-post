Do gender quotas make sense?

Recently I've heard a lot about them because of statements by some public figures who called them useless and even discriminatory against men.
And I was surprised not to see posts about it here on LinkedIn, since in the end it's a topic that concerns work and we're on the social network of work par excellence.

I admit I went looking for more information on a subject I had never really looked into, and this is the idea I came away with.

I agree they're an unfair law but a necessary one to fight an unfair system in which women struggle (or used to?) in particular to enter places of power, whether political or corporate.

So maybe the real question to ask isn't whether they're fair but whether they're still necessary today.

If I had to base it on my personal experience I honestly wouldn't know how to answer.

But going by the statistics, it would seem so, since in Italy women are still underrepresented in top roles.

What do you think?
I'd be curious to read the opinion of other women in particular.
