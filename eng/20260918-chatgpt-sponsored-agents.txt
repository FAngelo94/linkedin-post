OpenAI has started testing sponsored agents inside ChatGPT.

Since September 16th, in the United States and only for some selected advertisers, when a "relevant" ad appears you can open a conversation with an agent of the company.
You ask it questions, it answers, and in the end it takes you to the website.

In the same announcement:
- ad titles and descriptions that adapt on their own to the context of the conversation, translated into your language
- HubSpot first CRM partner, Shopify first ecommerce partner
- campaigns created and monitored without leaving HubSpot

The problem?
For twenty years advertising sat next to the result.
You recognized it by its position, its box, the word "Sponsored".

Here it has the same face and the same tone of voice as whoever was answering you a moment before.
The only difference is a label.

Like the shop assistant who recommends, "as a friend", exactly the product on promotion.

OpenAI assures that this conversation is separate from the original one and distinct from ChatGPT's independent answers.
I believe it, of course.
But it's the same OpenAI that in August kept billing for ads up to a day after the campaign was paused, because that's what the terms said.

Would you notice the label?
And if you work in marketing, are you already giving it some thought?
