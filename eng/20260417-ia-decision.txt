Recently on LinkedIn I see more and more posts about how much decision-making power to give AI in projects, and about the importance of human oversight to avoid harm, especially in cybersecurity.

But then you look up and discover that, in other parts of the world, AI doesn't limit itself to suggesting code or optimizing processes.
It is starting to enter decisions that have real, irreversible and, perhaps, questionable consequences.

I'm not writing this to say what's right or wrong — I'm not even sure myself.
But the news made me reflect on how quickly the boundary is shifting between what is acceptable to leave to AI and what instead requires closer human control.
