Traveling with a baby has its advantages too.

Before my son was born, when I imagined traveling with him, I only thought about the new difficulties.

These days, traveling in Japan, I've also discovered several advantages.

- At the airport we basically don't queue anymore, there's a dedicated lane
- At some attractions, like several temples, there are dedicated entrances: no long line and we enjoy the visit much more
- On public transport, as long as I hold him in my arms, a seat always turns up 😂

The difficulties are there, of course.
But everyone already talks about those.
