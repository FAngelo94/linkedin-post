How did AWS Summit 2026 go?
Is it worth attending?

It was my first time and I went without any particular expectations. 
Here's the idea I got of the event.

👍 Things I liked:
1. The overview of new AWS services and upcoming news.
2. The chance to discover existing services that, for one reason or another, I hadn't explored yet.
3. The chance to meet AWS partner companies working on interesting projects.
4. A huge amount of ideas that will probably end up in my upcoming LinkedIn posts.
5. Free gadgets and stickers. Always welcome. 😄

👎 Things that convinced me less:
1. Many companies seemed more focused on announcing that they use AI than on explaining the real value of their products or services.
2. I found fewer networking opportunities than I expected.
