Meet the Neurosleeper, the device that halves your sleep

You know how some animals (like dolphins or certain seabirds) sleep with only one hemisphere of the brain at a time? 
Well, the Neurosleeper simulates this mechanism to let humans sleep with just one half of the brain, while the other stays active.

Which allows us to be "productive" (and consumers) 24 hours a day.

Unfortunately, or fortunately, the Neurosleeper is pure fiction for now.

It's the plot of the interesting book "Non dormire e sogna" ("Don't sleep and dream"), written by Giuseppe De Alteriis, who happens to be a neuroscientist too.

I found the concept fascinating though. There are some days when I wouldn't mind not having to sleep in order to get more done. What do you think?
