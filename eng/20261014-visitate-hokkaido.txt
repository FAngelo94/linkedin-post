Sapporo was the biggest surprise of my trip to Japan.

Curiosities from Japan - part 3

On this second trip I dedicated half of the time to Hokkaido, starting from Sapporo, its main city.

I had high expectations for Hokkaido and they were met: from the walk with a view of Mount Yotei (known as the "Mount Fuji of Hokkaido") to the hot springs of Noboribetsu.

From Sapporo, instead, I didn't expect much.
Having already seen Tokyo and Osaka, I thought it was just a city to pass through.

Instead it turned out to be a very fascinating city:
- Decidedly less chaotic than Tokyo and Osaka, wherever we went it wasn't crowded
- Everything reachable on foot, if you book the hotel in the right place
- And, as I told you in the previous post, they accepted cards practically everywhere

And if, like me, you love manga, anime and video games, in Sapporo I found the best shops, well organized and with very good prices.
First of all Mandarake, the best of the ones I visited in Japan.

Those who voted in the poll a few days ago that they've already been to Japan: did you make it all the way to Hokkaido or did you stop at Tokyo and Osaka?
And those who haven't been yet, would you put it on your plans?
