Last week I read a post asking for advice on an office chair.

I perfected my workstation over a few years, one piece at a time. Some things you don't realize how comfortable they are until you use them every day for months.

Where would I start today?

- Ergonomic office chair, not a gaming one, adjustable backrest and breathable fabric
- Height-adjustable desk. The motor has to be bought, but for the top here's a trick: a second-hand kitchen table to take apart. It cost me less than a raw plank from the DIY store
- Excellent monitor, it's the thing you'll stare at most in your life. I have a single 32-inch with a blue light filter, some people prefer two small screens
- Articulated arm to adjust the monitor as you like, fixed to the edge of the desk
- Sliding tray for keyboard and mouse, useful to position both the monitor and the arms at the right height

For an optional extra upgrade?

- Mechanical keyboard: typing is better and it's much easier to clean since the keys come off one by one
- Ergonomic mouse. I have two, a traditional one and a vertical one that I alternate periodically
- Wrist rests for both mouse and keyboard, maybe the most underrated thing of all
- Cable tray for freer legs

And to avoid sitting still all day?

- Yoga ball, to change position while staying seated
- Balance board, I admit even I didn't remember the name: an unstable round board you stand on while working

I'm also considering a desk treadmill.

What would you add?
