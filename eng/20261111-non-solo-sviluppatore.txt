Don't let yourself be defined by your job title.

It's advice I recently heard in a podcast about personal branding.

I share it but I find it hard to apply here on LinkedIn.
Where another piece of advice I often hear is to specialize and sell yourself in a small, specific niche.

But niches feel tight to me.

I'm not just a Fullstack Developer.
I'm Angelo Falci and I also work as a Fullstack Developer.

I've been developing for more than 15 years and I've worked on a huge variety of projects such as:
- complex websites
- cross-platform mobile app development
- multiplayer PC games
- data analysis and implementation of recommendation algorithms
- building and developing robotic prototypes
- technical and strategic consulting for some startups
- D&D dungeon master (ok now I'm drifting too far xD)

Ok, but what's all this variety for, for whoever hires me?

An example. 
The robots built at university didn't earn me a single euro.
Years later a robotics startup was looking for someone for the software and it was a field in which I had already banged my head in the past.

When a client brings me a problem, it's rarely the first time I see something similar.
And when they hire me for a specific problem I can usually help them with other things that come up while working together.

And you? Do you recognize yourselves in a single title?
And if you're on the other side, when you hire or choose a supplier, do you look at the title or the background?
