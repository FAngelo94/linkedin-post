Have you heard of RentAHuman.ai?

The site works roughly like this:
- Humans sign up with skills, city and hourly rate.
- AI agents search the database, select a profile and assign a task.
- Payments are made in cryptocurrencies via protocols like MCP.

In other words, the situation is reversed: it’s no longer us using AI but AI using us!

Will we become tools of AI Agents?
Maybe later we’ll be fired when they have robots to upload themselves into?

Jokes aside, at the moment the site isn’t widely used and I think it’s more of a social experiment than a business that will explode in the future, but never say never
