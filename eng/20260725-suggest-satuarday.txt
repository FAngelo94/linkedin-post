Unsolicited Saturday tip for those starting to use AI seriously 👇

If you're approaching AI tools for your work for the first time, avoid the courses that after five minutes already start talking about skills, sub-agents, MCP, frameworks and a thousand other concepts.

Just write a simple prompt asking for what you need in as much detail as possible and get familiar with the tool this way

Today many models are so advanced that, in most cases, a good prompt is more than enough to get quality results

Only one tip: download the tool locally, don't use it from the browser, so that it can interact with your files

Only afterwards would I start exploring everything else, maybe one tool at a time

Otherwise the risk is getting confused, configuring the tools badly, wasting tokens and even coming to think that AI "doesn't work", when in reality you're just adding complexity too early.

Change my mind if you disagree.

P.S. The idea for this post came to me after recently following, out of curiosity, a video course on the subject
