Have you ever wondered how Captchas work?

The old ones were simple: distorted text, you rewrote it and you passed.
But you might not know that with reCAPTCHA v1, Google used your answers to digitize books. Millions of people working for free without knowing it.

Then came the famous "I'm not a robot", and that was the first time I went looking for info on captchas because I couldn't understand how it could work.
Why does clicking a checkbox prove I'm not a bot? I can do that with some JavaScript.
The system analyzes how you move the mouse, the speed, the tiny oscillations of the cursor. A human has imprecise and random movements, a bot doesn't, unless...
The real check actually starts pages before you reach that checkbox, in short.

But the most interesting version is reCAPTCHA v3, which you probably haven't seen yet.
It's the invisible one 😂
It doesn't ask you anything, it runs in the background and assigns you a score from 0 to 1.
To do that it collects various data like IP address, cookies, mouse movements, keys pressed, device information.

All sent to Google's servers.

Basically, to prove you're human, you give Google a full package of your online behaviors.
Not surprisingly, reCAPTCHA v3 has several GDPR compliance issues.
