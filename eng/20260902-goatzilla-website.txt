A new gym recently opened near my home.

Knowing in advance, when it was still under construction and they didn't have a website, I called them to offer to build it.

Since it would be a showcase site that I'd build quickly, I wouldn't even have asked for money.
I'd have tried to barter the site for a membership of a length to be determined xD

But we didn't even get to talking about price, they immediately told me they already had someone taking care of it.

Then the site came out.

The result?

Oversized components that force you to scroll constantly.

And it's not even responsive. 

Absurd stuff in 2026 when most people browse from their phone and don't even own a PC.

I'm curious whether they at least got it for free.
Maybe I'll ask when I go to try the gym.
