What are the most important tests to write in a project?

It's a question I was asked every now and then in the past in some interviews.

There are so many types of tests that if I tried to list them I'd surely forget some.

My answer today?
The one you don't write.

Now that AI is increasingly able to write and run lots of them automatically, the most important test remains the human one.

Opening the site and trying the various features by hand.

The reason?
Behind it there are often problems that AI, at the moment, isn't able to detect:
- a button that's too awkward to click because of its position on the page
- no visual feedback when you interact with an element
- a navigation flow that isn't intuitive 
- an element that's too small or too big for the page context

All things that don't break the software, but that can have a big impact on the user experience.
