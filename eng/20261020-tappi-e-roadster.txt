These days I've seen quite a few memes going around with the same comparison.

On one side the United States with the Tesla Roadster that flies.
On the other Europe with the caps attached to the bottles.

They innovate, we regulate, in short.

I want to play the contrarian.
When we talk about innovation we always look at the thing that flies, but some innovations go by more quietly.
The Roadster will be affordable for a lucky few, assuming it actually arrives.
The cap was one of the most common pieces of waste found on beaches and in the seas.

One is spectacle, the other is a small thing that nonetheless makes a difference for many.

What do you think?

PS: innovation happens in Europe too, you just have to look into it a bit.
Often, though, in fields that generate less hype, but not for that less important for the community. On the contrary.
