They scanned 159,041 apps made with Lovable: 24,664 returned the database data without asking for a login.

What was inside?
- 7,403 apps with tables of email addresses
- over 1,000 records with passport number, nationality and expiry date
- payment data
- about 200,000 chat messages

The cause in most cases? A switch turned off on the database, Row Level Security, the thing that says "this row can only be read by whoever is entitled to it".

The problem had already come out in 2025, and even today Lovable says that security is a shared responsibility between them and whoever publishes the app.
