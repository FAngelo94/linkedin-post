I increasingly encounter people, non-developers or junior developers, who developed something with the help of AI but have no idea how or where to publish it.
For an MVP, or generally for a site or webapp that I want to publish at zero cost, or almost, my preferred choice is Firebase Hosting.

- Easy to configure and integrates effortlessly with GitHub Actions
- Has a very generous free tier
- Offers a set of ready-made services, if needed, like database, authentication, serverless functions, etc.
