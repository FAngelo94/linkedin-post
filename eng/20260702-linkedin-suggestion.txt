Cultivate your LinkedIn network.

It's the advice I'd give myself if I could go back to when I created my LinkedIn profile.

Over the years, out of ignorance/inexperience, I accepted anyone who sent me a connection request, without evaluating whether they could be useful contacts or not.

But now that as a freelancer I use LinkedIn more strategically, I realize that the quality of my network is much more important than the quantity.

Because the posts you publish are seen first of all by the people you're connected with, so having a network of contacts relevant to your industry or professional interests increases the chances that your content gets seen and appreciated.

In the past few months I've been doing two things:

- removing contacts that no longer make sense for me, especially foreigners living on the other side of the world;
- when I receive a new request, I first look at the profile and, if I accept, I immediately write a message to understand who I'm dealing with and why they want to connect.

I should have done it sooner.
But better late than never.
