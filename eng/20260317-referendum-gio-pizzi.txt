The importance of priorities - part 2

You have an e-commerce that's in bad shape.
Bugs everywhere, slow, half of the payments don't work, registrations fail randomly.

You hire a professional and he starts telling you how important it would be to redesign the layout of the pages and fix the theme colors.

Well, I feel like the owner of that e-commerce.
And the politicians with the current referendum seem to me like that professional.

Two things:
- Those who govern us don't know how to identify priorities (because clearly the referendum is not a priority for the majority of Italians)
- Those who govern us think about their own priorities, not those of the public

What do you think?
