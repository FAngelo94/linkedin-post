A robot broke Bolt's 100 meter record.

How many of you read this news?

I came across quite a few posts here on LinkedIn, and newspaper articles too, that told it by focusing everything on the pointlessness of beating a human record.
As if that were the goal of these robot Olympics.

But it isn't.

These races between robots don't exist to beat humans over 100 meters.

To me it seems obvious, but I think it isn't for everyone, especially for those completely outside the tech or robotics world.

What are they really for?

To test humanoid robots in realistic contexts, where they have to interact with the environment and complete complex tasks.

They're a testing ground for companies in the sector: they test prototypes by measuring themselves against competitors and collect valuable data to improve their robots, on both the software and hardware sides.

And Italy?

It took part through academic channels, mainly using pre-assembled robots rather than its own.
The goal of the Italian labs was to test their own software.

Bolt's record, in all this, is the color piece for newspapers. Not the point.
