The underrated art of making yourself useless

When I work for a client, my ultimate goal is to become useless.

It sounds like a silly thing to do, someone might think.

What do I mean? 
In work, as in life, I value independence.
In the same way I want to make my clients independent from me.
I want them to keep working with me because they recognize the value, not because they're forced to.

How?
- I hand over all the code I produce.
- Service accounts are in the client's name, not mine.
- If there's an internal team, I leave support and documentation to make them autonomous.

These sound like obvious basics, but they aren't.
I've talked with several people who are hostages, one way or another, of other professionals' work.

What do you think?
Should a professional aim to make themselves indispensable or replaceable?
