These days I'm playing Zelda: Ocarina of Time.
The remake of course, I had never played the original.

A fantastic game, which I can't help looking at with a developer's eyes too.

One of the ideas I like most?
A single button that changes action depending on where you are.
In front of a door you open it, next to a pot you lift it, next to a character you talk to them.
The game offers you the only thing that makes sense to do at that moment.

I adopted a similar approach for a client's web app.

His web app had a page that did a bit of everything.
Several features one below the other, buttons everywhere.
And they used it mainly from their phone.

I split it into several pages, each with a single task and a single main button, clearly visible.
Whoever opens it knows right away what they can do there, without searching.

It looks like a step backward: more pages instead of one.
But on a small screen having everything within reach often means not finding anything.

Do you also have a page, in the site or the management software you use every day, where you have to hunt for the right button every time?
