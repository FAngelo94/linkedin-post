The project I'm most proud of?
It's certainly among the projects that have given me the most satisfaction in my working career, at least so far.

In short, Smanno is a startup that develops automation solutions for clinical laboratories, using primarily collaborative robotics both fixed and mobile, and it's currently officially entering the market selling its first products.

On my side I was lucky to be involved in this project from the start and I built the entire software infrastructure that allows managing the robots (the development of the individual robots and the hardware is the work of Emanuele Schiavon)

We're talking about:
- database design
- backend development (API + direct integration with the robots)
- frontend development (responsive web app with role management and advanced interactions)
- versioning and management of installation on clients' systems

The main objective was to create a software platform that was as flexible as possible so as to be able to manage robotics solutions and very different scenarios and to create complex tasks in a simple way.

A result that so far has been achieved and that we are continuing to improve day after day, also thanks to feedback from our customers.

Why did it give me so much satisfaction?
Because it's a project, of a certain complexity, that I brought to life and of which I took care of every single aspect independently.
