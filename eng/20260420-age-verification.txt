Out of curiosity I went to look at the repo, since it's open source:
https://github.com/eu-digital-identity-wallet

And at first glance it looks like a mess with its 81 repositories and scattered documentation everywhere.

Why so many repositories?
Because Age Verification is not an isolated project.
It's only a piece of a much larger system on European digital identity.

I like the idea of open source.
I wonder how sustainable a project of this size really is, when its structure is so fragmented that it reflects even in external communication.
