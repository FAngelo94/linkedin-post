Studying AWS by playing cards?
You can!

One of the most interesting things I came across at the AWS Summit was AWS Card Clash, a 1 vs 1 card game where you discover AWS services in a fun, interactive way.

In short:
- Each player gets a deck where every card represents an AWS service.
- In front of you is the diagram of a real AWS architecture and you have to place the cards in the right spots to complete it.
- The more cards you place correctly, the more points you earn.
- If, at the end of your turn or your opponent's, the architecture is complete, the game ends and whoever has more points wins.

Also every card has a question mark that, when clicked, explains the details of that AWS service, helping you learn while playing even if you're a beginner.

I tried it and I must say it's very well made.. 
They could do the same for other topics/services.
