LinkedIn has turned off its AI.

In recent days I read a lot of posts about the new button that lets you report low-quality AI-generated content.

Only today though did I discover the other half of the news: LinkedIn also removed "Enhance your post", the feature that rewrote your posts with AI.

It was reserved for premium users, so it went by pretty quietly.

And I find it a curious choice.

If the goal is to fight badly written AI content, removing your own tool doesn't help much. 

Did they remove it because it didn't work well or because it was too expensive to maintain?

I lean toward the second.

What do you think? Had you ever used it as a feature?
