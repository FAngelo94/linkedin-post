Time doesn't pass at the same speed for everyone

Haven't you ever had the feeling that now that you work time passes faster than when you were younger, maybe a student? 

This happens because when you're young everything is new, the brain spends more energy recording unfamiliar facts, stretching the perception of time.

As adults, instead, everything is more familiar, there are established routines and the brain glides quickly over habitual facts, making days seem shorter.

Today I turn 32. And in the last year the opposite happened to me.

- Buying a house
- Opening a VAT number
- Birth of my son

To mention the main ones, which alone are enough.

Result: it's the year that has felt the longest since I finished studying.

How do you remember your last year? Long or flew by?
