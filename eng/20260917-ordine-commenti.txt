LinkedIn has changed the order of comments under posts

There's no longer one order for everyone.
Each reader sees a ranking calculated on themselves: professional interests, connections, engagement activity.

Announced in August 2026, rolling out since then

The official reason?
Time spent reading comments has grown 18% year over year and LinkedIn wants "more relevant" conversations

It should be said that according to statistics AI-generated comments are becoming more and more common.
So it could also be a strategy to give more visibility to real comments and hide AI-generated ones.

Graphically nothing has changed, you'll still see the "Most relevant" option to sort comments, the algorithm underneath has changed.
Before, it simply showed the most viewed comments at the top.

I'm personally not convinced it's a positive change.

The upside is that it could give more visibility to comments that are truly interesting for each user rather than to the most popular one.

The downside is that it could make it harder to discover interesting comments that don't fall within our immediate interests, perhaps losing the chance to change perspective and/or learn something new.
