Do in-depth technical interviews make sense?

While I was an employee they seemed normal to me.
Okay, there's a probationary period, but usually you hire someone already thinking they'll stay.
I've never heard of someone not passing it (except for a very particular case).

Since I started working as a freelancer, however, they seem like a waste of time to me.

When I interact with a new client, the first thing I try to understand is whether I have the right skills for that project.
If I understand that I'm not able, I'm the first to say it.
Even if I have little experience with something and would need more time than usual, I communicate it openly.

For this reason I find it pointless to waste time in technical interviews where I'm given "the exam."
Explain the project to me and I'll tell you directly if I have the skills, maybe telling you how I intend to approach it.

Besides, with the subscription model I offer, we don't sign any contract.
If you are unhappy after a while, you can simply stop the service on the spot.

What do you think?

I'd like to know the opinion both of other freelancers and people on the other side: clients who interact with freelancers or HR/head hunters who have experiences to share on the topic.
