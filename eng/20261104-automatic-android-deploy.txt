Do you know what one of the most boring activities in development is, to me?
Deploying what I develop.

Especially if it's an Android app.

So I decided to automate everything for Autochess of Ages, the game I've already told you about.

I thought the permissions part would be the most complicated.
Instead all it took was creating a service account on Google Cloud, giving it the right permissions and "inviting" it to the project on Google Play Console.

Then I saved everything needed in GitHub Secrets: keystore, passwords and service account credentials.

The last piece was done by Claude.
I explained the whole flow to it and it generated the yml file for GitHub Actions.

I admit that, on my own, this would have been the most complex part.

Now?
I push a tag and after a few minutes the new version is on the Play Store.

Those who develop mobile, have you automated the release too or do you still upload everything by hand?
