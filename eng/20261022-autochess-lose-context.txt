Autochess of Ages didn't win any prize.

And actually I expected it xD

For those who missed the post where I talked about it:
at the end of August, through an ad banner, I discover a hackathon on a site called Devpost.

The goal?
Create an app of any kind that used the RevenueCat library to handle some payment.

I use the hackathon as an excuse to try to create a mobile game and enter it.
For development I dusted off several old acquaintances and, above all, learned new ones.

I also managed to involve a couple of people, one I met by chance here on LinkedIn, who made development more fun.

What came out of it?
A fun little game, although far from perfect at the moment.
This, together with the enormous number of projects entered in the contest, made it hard to hope to win.

But oh well, I'll try to carry it forward anyway in my free time since I had fun developing it.

For those who want to try it I'm leaving the link in the comments 👇
And if you like it and want to take part in development, feel free to write to me!
