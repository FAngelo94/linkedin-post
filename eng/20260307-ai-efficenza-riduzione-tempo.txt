Can AI make us work less? 🤖⏳

After reading about the rejection of the proposal for a 32-hour workweek, this question came back to me.

When we talk about AI in software development, the focus is almost always the same:
more efficiency, more productivity, more features released.

But if we can do in fewer hours what we used to do in 8…
why do we keep working 8 (if not more)?

I came up with two possible main explanations:
- If a single company reduces hours, it risks losing competitiveness compared to those who keep producing more.
- If a State imposes the reduction from above, it risks losing companies or competitiveness compared to countries that don't, in an increasingly global market.

The result?
The increase in productivity is almost always reinvested in more output, not more free time.
Output that, in some sectors, also translates into waste.

I don't see simple solutions.
We would need broad coordination between countries and companies… something that, looking at the current climate, seems rather far off.

So the question remains open:
Will we be able in the next few years to use AI also to work less, and not only to produce more? 🤔
