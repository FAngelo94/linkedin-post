Oracle Lays Off Employees to Invest in AI

By now it's not even news in itself, many big companies are laying people off under the pretext of AI, but what struck me most in this case is the way it was done.

Employees were notified by email on the morning of their last day.
Imagine waking up, reading the email and discovering it's your last day of work, just like that.

A company with an AI as CEO, I think, would have handled it with more humanity.
